About the map and its data

Draft. Nothing here has been checked by a historian. Borders before about 1500 are approximate, and for the earliest centuries the data holds only a few named peoples; many regions are unnamed. Each century shows the nearest available snapshot, and its year is written on screen.

  • Historical borders: historical-basemaps (aourednik and contributors), GPL-3.0, simplified for the web. Its latest snapshot is 2010, so the “Today” stop shows 2010 borders.
  • Coastline: Natural Earth, public domain.
  • Map engine: MapLibre GL JS, BSD-3-Clause.
  • Wars shown: a curated list, each with a page to check it against. Highlights show roughly where a war was fought, not its exact extent.
